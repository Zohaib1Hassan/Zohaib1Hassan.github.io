Mini Scissor Lift

2.0minimum safety factor on link bending and pin bearing stress

  • Designed a screw-driven scissor lift in CAD for 3D printing, with 8 parts and a 64 mm to 127 mm lifting stroke.
  • Modeled the lift's kinematics and statics, finding peak screw force of about 243 N at a 9 kg design load.
  • Checked link bending and pin bearing stress against PLA strength, reaching a minimum safety factor of 2.0.
  • Sized an M3 lead screw and calculated the torque and about 105 knob turns needed to raise the full stroke.
  • Verified the force model against the W/tan(θ) equilibrium relationship and documented all design assumptions.

Mechatronics

4-DOF Servo Arm

150poses it can record and replay, with all four joints arriving together

  • Assembled a 4-DOF robotic arm from 3D-printed parts, driven by four SG90 servos for base, shoulder, wrist and gripper.
  • Wrote Arduino C++ firmware mapping four potentiometers (10-bit ADC) to servo pulse widths at a 40 Hz update rate.
  • Implemented record-and-replay of up to 150 poses, using eased interpolation so all four joints reach each pose together.
  • Calibrated per-joint pulse-width limits to prevent servo stall at end stops and diagnosed power and wiring faults.
